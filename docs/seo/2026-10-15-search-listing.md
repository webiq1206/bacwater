## Measurement and outstanding scope

The research/growth owner should check crawl and snippet integrity around seven days after verified publication, then compare complete equal windows around 30 and 60 days. Compare page clicks and CTR with position, country, device and query mix where available. Keep the converter host rows distinct in source receipts and avoid treating them as independent audiences. Respect anonymized or missing query rows and the small baseline. Prefer qualified calculator completion where privacy-safe receiving evidence is available; a search click alone is not a completed calculation or business outcome.

Revert or revise the experiment if it produces misleading snippets or loses intent fit. CTR changes without comparable position and query mix do not establish causality. There is no fixed ranking, citation or traffic guarantee. These review points are a plan, not new scheduled automations or completed future checks.
