## Preserve and follow up

Retain existing evidence limits, source links, affiliate disclosures, server-rendered content, unique share images, consistent favicon, typed calculators, privacy gates and corrected canonicals. Do not add invented ratings, offers, credentials, review dates, local addresses or thin keyword pages. FAQ markup does not guarantee eligibility or a rich result.

Google's AI guidance requires the same foundations as ordinary search. No special AI schema or llms.txt work is justified by this audit. Existing AI-crawler settings were preserved; search access and training controls are different decisions.

Use the deployment date as day zero. Verify metadata, source citations, headings, search opening/focus, purchase links, consent behavior and forms immediately after publication. Check sitemap processing and a representative URL set after 7 to 14 days. Compare the first complete 28 days after deployment with the preceding 28 days, using equivalent weekday coverage and final data. Segment page/query/device/country and explicit brand terms; retain privacy-suppression caveats and aggregate historical www/apex variants for trend analysis.

Prioritize qualified calculator completions, successful plan saves, contact receipts and disclosed outbound supplier clicks. Outbound clicks are not purchases. Do not infer conversion rates, AI citations, users or revenue from search clicks. GA receiving-property configuration, consent collection and Clarity access must be verified before behavioral conclusions. Core Web Vitals require field data; fast unthrottled browser navigation is not a substitute. PageSpeed public requests were quota-limited during the audit.

The contact form stores a support message but does not implement an automatic email notification. A notification workflow requires a verified destination and a configured delivery service. Authenticated account recovery, cross-device ownership, partner checkout and notification delivery remain separate acceptance checks.
