# Calculator clarity and product research

The reviewed source baseline is 2b27d9e3f8348ed7f213d5d112e7298d07b5e516. The user's two Library PNGs were materialized on the authorized Mac and their pixels inspected. They showed three decisions grouped in the amount screen, a native gray/blue menu, and amount-meaning help beneath the schedule control.

The default calculator now asks one question at a time. Unit choice is separate from the number; amount meaning, amount and schedule are separate questions. Back retains entries. Changing amount meaning clears the amount and schedule so a per-time number cannot silently become a total. Help sits beside “This amount is for.” Secondary explanations, name and date stay collapsed. Name and date use mutually exclusive disclosure groups, so only one optional input is shown at a time.

The shared dropdown uses the site's existing dark green, cream, soft green and Montserrat tokens. It preserves Radix keyboard selection, restores trigger focus, isolates the hidden page while open and uses a focusable choices group for scrolling. Menu sizing follows the available viewport; controls are at least 48px tall.

Single compounds, product IU, premixed blends, prepared liquids and water totals retain separate math. GLOW asks for all three ingredients; KLOW asks for all four. No ratios or amounts are supplied. Concentration-only calculations skip irrelevant sample questions. Saved edits remain isolated from the active tab calculation. Existing advanced layout is retained for explicit users of that view.

Spray summaries retain explicit finished-liquid limits, blend summaries retain ingredient-only limits, and related-form/background sources are labeled.
