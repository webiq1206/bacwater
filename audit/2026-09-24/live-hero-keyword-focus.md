# Live homepage calculator and keyword alignment

The user requested a functional hero calculator, a little more visual depth, preservation of the full-height opening, and evidence-based keyword targeting.

## Implementation

The homepage leads with BAC Water Calculator in its visible H1, HTML title, social metadata and software schema. The title is `BAC Water Calculator | Peptide Reconstitution | BACwater.ai`. Peptide reconstitution calculator is a related secondary phrase in the visible description and an original formula explainer. These are descriptive labels for an actual tool, not promises of rankings or treatment guidance.

The restored editorial composition now contains live concentration, mass and U-100 volume calculations. Blank initial values, explicit example loading, validation, optional user-entered measurement, mass-preserving unit switching, copy/reset and per-tab session persistence replace the static example. Mobile interaction opens the same state in a full-viewport, keyboard-accessible dialog. Existing guided pages are retained. Original product artwork, all 50 catalog choices, direct product links and the separate water link remain available in the focused tool. No product-linked dose or dilution is selected.

## Keyword decision

Research combined the owner's private Search Console report, live search-result intent and Google Search Central guidance. Site-specific impressions do not measure total search volume. Private query metrics are not published in this repository. No verified market-wide monthly volume, competitive-difficulty score or conversion-value ranking was available. BAC water calculator is the best-supported current homepage target, not a proven highest-volume term. Broader peptide reconstitution intent remains a major secondary opportunity. Generic peptide calculator also has molecular-property intent; individual tools and guides retain distinct jobs and URLs.

References checked: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link ; https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content ; https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features ; https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553 . No special AI schema or guaranteed placement is claimed.

No database/schema, dependency, deployment safety, supplier signup or paid tracking change is part of this update. Replit publication is separate from Git delivery.
